Its state for whoever opens the page - the amounts the allocation gives THIS invoice, oldest first, never beyond what it asks. With « Banque » only: the credits paying it with their shares, those that could - one form per option holding this invoice, each naming every invoice it posts with what it still asks (a sum linked as one invoice would leave the other out in silence, and a sum's reason names none) -, and a search of the credits. Every form posts to Banque's own action (bank:bank_line_action, the « Banque » gate) and comes back here, its message said in this section (`lieu`). Each carries `data-leaves-lines`: it leaves what was typed on the document, and static/js/sale_document.js asks first. The search is a GET form: it carries the tab's parameters as hidden fields, a browser dropping the action's own query string. {% endcomment %}
